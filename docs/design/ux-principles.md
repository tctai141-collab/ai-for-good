# Sprint Buddy — UX Principles

## Core UX Challenge

> If participants feel monitored, they'll perform instead of reflect.
> Solve this and you've solved the hard part.

## Principles

1. **Safe space first** — Everything starts from: "will the founder be honest here?" If not, redesign.
2. **Coach, not dashboard** — The primary relationship is founder → buddy, not organizer → buddy.
3. **Reflection, not transaction** — Check-ins aren't forms. They're conversations that help founders notice what they're avoiding.
4. **Private by default** — What's shared with organizers must be explicit, opt-in, and clearly communicated.
5. **Patterns over data** — Don't show raw answers. Show themes, shifts, and insights over time.
6. **Voice matters** — Tone should feel like a seasoned operator who's been through it, not a therapist or a robot.

## Design References

- Not a chatbot (generic) — a coach (specific personality)
- Not a survey tool — a companion
- Not a dashboard — a signal
