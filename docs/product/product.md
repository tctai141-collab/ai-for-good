# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Aalto Founder Sprint participants: founders inside a 15-week intense program working on trend research, product validation, and company-building. They use Sprint Buddy at decision moments. Late at night, around 11pm, in a dim room. Before a hard co-founder conversation. After a tough pitch. In the moments where stress is high and clarity is low. Their context is private. They need a space that helps them think, not one that watches them think. They are time-poor, sleep-poor, and shoulder-tap-poor. They do not need another dashboard.

A second audience uses the same product from a different door: program organizers and lead coaches. They see emotional temperature across the cohort, never raw transcripts. They use it to decide who to reach out to, before the founder has to ask.

## Product Purpose

Sprint Buddy gives a founder honest, grounded guidance from a calm, scarred, generous founder voice they can turn to. The product is three surfaces.

A private chat that meets the founder where they are: panicking, thinking, or venting. The assistant adapts its posture to the arriving state, names reversible vs one-way decisions, and slows things down when nothing has to be decided tonight.

A Reflections view that quietly mirrors what the data shows about their week, in plain language, without performance pressure.

A coach view that surfaces emotional temperature across the cohort and gives the coach the right thing to open a conversation with, while leaving the founder's words untouched.

Success is the founder closing the laptop feeling steadier, with one clear next step.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Human, Unpolished. A coach who has been in the trenches. Energetic but not hype-y. Kind but not soft. Direct, warm, grounded in real operator experience. Never corporate, never clinical.

The product also lives inside an Aalto identity context: rigorous, curious, primary-coloured, unafraid of saying things plainly. Two voices live together. Warm at the founder. Institutional at the brand surface. They share a palette and a typeface stack. They do not share a tone.

## Anti-references

- Generic chatbot UIs (the AI-cream + accent-blue look).
- Corporate wellness dashboards (BetterUp, Headspace for Work).
- Survey tools (Typeform, SurveyMonkey).
- Hero-metric / KPI templates: the big-number-small-label cliché.
- Pastel SaaS dashboards that take a brand palette and soften it to invisibility.
- Therapy-app aesthetics: soft gradients, rounded-everything, calming pastels.
- Bank-app gray-gradient CTAs.
- AI-slop signatures: gradient text, glassmorphism by default, side-stripe accent borders, identical card grids, "AI" cream + accent-purple.

## Design Principles

1. **Wear Aalto, do not whisper it.** Aalto Blue, Red, and Yellow at full saturation, used confidently. Never softened to pastels. Never blurred to invisibility. The visual language commits the way Aalto's brand commits: primary color, plain type around it, generous space. This is an Aalto tool for Aalto founders; the identity should be visible from the first pixel and present, in some named form, on every surface.

2. **Coach in pocket, not a dashboard.** The primary relationship is founder → buddy. Every UI decision should make the founder feel helped, not tracked. When in doubt, ask whether a coach would do this, or whether a CRM would do this. Pick the coach.

3. **Privacy as architecture.** If founders feel monitored, they perform instead of reflect. Privacy is not a setting; it is the shape of the system. The coach view never sees raw transcripts. Themes and trends only. Organizer signals are explicit, named, and scoped.

4. **Two voices, one product.** Warm and unpolished at the founder: Sprint Buddy speaking in chat, the empty state, the soft check-in. Rigorous and clear at the brand: login, marketing, institutional moments. They share a palette and a stack. They do not share a tone.

5. **Earned authority, not generic startup advice.** The design conveys that the guidance comes from someone who has actually done it. Authenticity over polish. The serif voice on assistant replies, the reversible-vs-one-way frame, the "nothing has to be decided tonight" beat: these are the design.

6. **Dark by physical scene, not by reflex.** The founder uses this at 11pm under low ambient light, often on a phone, often alone, often anxious. Dark surface is the correct answer for that scene, not a category-default. Stay in the scene.

7. **Fast where founders are fast, slow where they need to be slowed.** Composer, send, navigation: instant. Decision frame, reflection sentence, soft check-in: deliberately slower, served on serif body, asking them to breathe. Motion and typography gate the pace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, with AAA where it is reasonable on body text.

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for all non-essential motion. The cube parallax, the landing transition, the rise animation: all suppressed under reduced-motion preference.

Color is never the sole semantic carrier. Cohort temperature cells pair color with a glyph or letter. Theme dots are always paired with a text label.

Touch targets meet 44 by 44 px minimum across the founder app. The late-night-on-phone scenario is canonical, not edge-case.

Helper text contrast holds at AA on the dark surface. The current `#6C6F78` faint color fails this and is being replaced.

Newsreader serif is reserved for assistant replies and chosen moments, never forced as a long-form body face. Inter remains the working face for any prose longer than two sentences.
