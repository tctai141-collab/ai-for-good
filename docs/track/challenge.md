# Founder OS — Aalto Founder Sprint Hackathon Challenge

> **Challenge by Aalto Founder Sprint** · Prize: €1,000 · Team size: up to 5 · Format: working prototype · Duration: 48 hours

## The Problem

Three weeks into the most intense founder program. It's 11pm. You have a hard conversation with your co-founder tomorrow and don't know how to frame it. A question you're embarrassed to ask your mentor. Not sure if what you're feeling is real signal or just noise.

**There's no one to call.** That gap exists in every cohort, every year.

## What We're Building: Sprint Buddy

An AI companion that travels with every Aalto Founder Sprint participant through their entire 15-week journey. Not a chatbot. Not a survey tool. A coach in their pocket — available at 11pm, before the hard conversation, in the silence after a bad pitch.

Starts with one advisor: **Mårten Mickos**, Head of Aalto Founder School, former CEO of MySQL and HackerOne. His actual frameworks, mental models, and hard-won lessons — distilled into a conversational AI.

## What to Build

### 1. The Mårten advisor (required)
A conversational AI loaded with Mårten's actual knowledge base. When a founder is stuck, they ask. The buddy responds with guidance grounded in real operator experience. Design so adding the next advisor is a matter of uploading new material.

### 2. Daily check-ins (required)
2–3 questions a day — the uncomfortable ones founders usually avoid. Not a form, not a survey. The buddy helps founders notice what they'd otherwise suppress. Over time, reflects patterns back: *"You've mentioned vision alignment three times this week — want to dig into that?"* Text or voice.

### 3. Founder profiling (stretch)
Help participants understand their strengths, blind spots, and best team role. Starting point: the 6 Types of Working Genius.

### 4. Organizer signal (stretch)
A weekly view of who might need attention. Human-in-the-loop. Participants must know what stays private vs what doesn't.

## The Hardest Design Problem

If participants feel monitored, they'll perform instead of reflect. The best solutions make founders *want* to be honest — because Sprint Buddy helps them, not just the organizers.
