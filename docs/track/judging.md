# Judging Criteria

**Judges:** Aalto Founder Sprint team
**Evaluation question:** Would we trust this with our next cohort?

Each criterion scored 1–5. Scores weighted and averaged. Max total: 5.0

| Criterion | Weight | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Founder experience | 30% | A founder would open this daily without being asked |
| AI integration | 25% | Guidance feels genuinely useful, not a glorified FAQ |
| Technical execution | 20% | Runs stably, demoed without breaking, clearly deployable |
| Extensibility | 15% | Adding a second advisor = uploading materials, not rebuilding |
| Wow factor | 10% | Makes the team say "we need this but didn't think of it" |

## Score Anchors

- **1** — Missing or broken
- **2** — Attempted but doesn't land
- **3** — Solid, does the job
- **4** — Strong, clearly thought through
- **5** — Exceptional — would deploy this tomorrow

Anything above **3.5** is genuinely strong work for 48 hours.
