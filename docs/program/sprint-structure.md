# Aalto Founder Sprint S26 — Program Overview

**Duration:** 15 weeks
**Format:** 3 sprints + study trip + weekly self-development

## Sprint 1 — Trend Research (Weeks 1–5)
Develop a data-driven "Thesis of the Future" — rigorous analysis of where the world is heading and where value will accrue. Starts with team bonding weekend. Includes desk research, expert interviews, scenario-building. Culminates in published trend report + presentation to critique panel.

## Sprint 2 — Product Validation (Weeks 6–10)
Goal: find evidence, not build. Test problem hypotheses with real users. Low- and high-fidelity prototyping. Iterate on feedback. Ends with pivot-or-persevere decision + MVP definition grounded in user insights.

## Week 11 — Ecosystem Trip Abroad
Visit startups, investors, and innovation hubs abroad. Networking, learning, team bonding outside Finland.

## Sprint 3 — Business & Strategy (Weeks 12–15)
Shift from product to company. Fundraising (angel, VC, public), go-to-market strategy, team culture, storytelling, co-founder dynamics. Closes with final pitch + graduation.

## Throughout — Weekly Self-Development
Each week pairs a personal growth theme (vulnerability, resilience, letting go) with a leadership theme (delegation, feedback, decision-making). Reflected on as a cohort every Tuesday morning.

**Guest sessions:** Every Wednesday and Thursday — founders, investors, operators across all 15 weeks.
